## Company overview

airpay helps businesses accept, make, and manage payments across online, offline, and cross-border channels. The platform brings together payment gateway, international payments, POS, EMI, UPI, payouts, refunds, settlements, reconciliation, developer APIs, and no-code commerce integrations.

| What airpay enables | How it helps |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept payments** | Accept payments across online and offline channels using cards, UPI, wallets, QR, POS, and more. |
| **Make payments** | Send payouts, refunds, and vendor payments with reliability and control. |
| **Manage payments** | Track transactions, settlements, reconciliation, and performance from a unified dashboard. |
| **Scale globally** | Support in-store, online, and cross-border payment flows from one platform. |
